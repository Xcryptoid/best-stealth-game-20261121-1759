The Gilded Archive — a top-down stealth game. Slip through a candlelit archive hall at night, steal the three star-charts, and leave by the green door unseen. Four wardens patrol with vision cones; shelves and crates break their sightlines, shadow patches slow their suspicion, and lantern light betrays you. Get spotted and the chase is on; get caught and the charts stay in the archive. Keyboard and mouse, no build step, no network — plain static files with synthesized sound.
